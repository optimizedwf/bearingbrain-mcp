# Code of Conduct

We use the Contributor Covenant as the baseline for this project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Report security issues privately rather than in public threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, spam, and repeated disruptive conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable moderation actions to protect the project community.
